About

From forest genomes to blood cancer, one sequence at a time.

I'm a doctoral researcher and bioinformatician at Universitätsmedizin / Universität Duisburg-Essen, working within the Department of Hematology and Stem Cell Transplantation at the West German Cancer Center (WTZ).

Since beginning my PhD in 2023, I have analysed single-cell sequencing data from myelofibrosis, acute myeloid leukemia, and experimental cell-line studies, including mouse models and human patient samples. I also develop R packages and reproducible bioinformatics workflows and provide analytical support to collaborating research groups.

My path began in forest genomics at the Forest Research Institute (ICFRE), Dehradun, where I worked on de novo genome assembly and molecular marker development. I hold a Master's degree in Bioinformatics from Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi.

Research focus

Five threads, one question: how does a cell decide what it becomes?

01

Single-cell RNA-seq

Resolving cell-state heterogeneity in progression and other hematological disease models.

02

NGS sequence analysis

Quality control, alignment, and de novo assembly across both clinical and non-model genomic datasets.

03

Structural bioinformatics

Linking sequence and structure to understand protein function and disease-relevant mutations.

04

Computer-aided drug discovery

Applying in-silico methods — docking and structure-based screening — to accelerate target identification.

05

Bioinformatics Tool Development

Development of bioinformatics functions and reproducible analysis workflows using R, Python, and Linux.
